\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Spot exchange rates}: daily noon buying rates in New York for the G10 currencies, Federal Reserve H.10 release, through FRED.
  \item \textbf{Interest rates}: short-term interbank rates (3-month) from the OECD Main Economic Indicators, through FRED; euro short-term rate (\texttt{ESTR}) from the ECB.
  \item \textbf{Forward rates}: not freely available; implied from spot and interest rates by CIP (equation~\ref{eq:rx-forward}). Limitation: post-2008 CIP deviations \citep{du2018}.
  \item \textbf{Transaction costs}: bid--ask spreads taken from the literature for G10 currencies \todo{reference and figures}.
\end{itemize}
